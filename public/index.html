BEFORE YOU VISIT

A few things
worth knowing.

Clear answers. A more comfortable visit.

Still have a question?
01

How can I arrange a consultation?

Call the helpline on +91 94108 99108 or send a WhatsApp enquiry using the form. The hospital team can confirm the doctor’s availability and appointment time. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

02

Is emergency care available at night?

Salus provides 24/7 emergency care. For urgent assistance, call +91 96967 96964. Do not wait for an email or an online appointment response in an emergency.

03

Can I use my health insurance?

The hospital offers insurance and cashless assistance. Eligibility depends on your insurer, policy, treatment and authorisation. Contact the team with your policy details before a planned admission.

04

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring your available medical reports, current prescriptions, a list of medicines, identification and insurance details if applicable. These are helpful preparations; do not delay urgent care to gather documents.

05

Are scans and blood tests available here?

The hospital has in-house radiology and pathology facilities, including CT, ultrasound, digital X-ray and laboratory investigations. Ask the team about timings and any preparation required for your particular test.

06

Where is Salus Hospitals located?

Near Raiya Circle, towards Raiya village, on Raiya Road in Rajkot. Use the Directions button for the supplied Google Maps location, or share the location card with your family.
